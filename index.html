📖 Instructions to Access Your USCIS Case JSON Data

Your case data lives in your own USCIS online account — this page can't fetch it for you. To pull it yourself:

1Log in at myaccount.uscis.gov/sign-in.
2In the same browser, open a new tab to:
https://my.uscis.gov/account/case-service/api/cases/IOE09XXXXXXXX
3Swap IOE09XXXXXXXX for your own receipt number (it starts with IOE).
4The tab will show raw JSON. Copy all of it (Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C), paste it into the box above, and hit Analyze Case.

The link only works while you're signed in, because your browser pulls the data straight from USCIS. This page never sees your login and cannot fetch anything on its own — everything you paste stays on your device.

⚠ Unofficial tool — not affiliated with USCIS or DHS · not legal advice · all processing stays in your browser

This is a personal, independent utility for viewing case data that you retrieve yourself from your own USCIS online account. It has no connection to U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the Department of Homeland Security, or any other government agency, law firm, or attorney, and nothing here has been reviewed or endorsed by any of them.

Everything on this page is computed inside your browser from the JSON you paste in. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere — close the tab and it's gone. The page's Content-Security-Policy blocks all outbound connections and form submissions, so the browser itself enforces that your data cannot be transmitted.
